Privacy vs Security

Privacy controls how personal information is collected, shared and exposed. Security protects accounts and systems from unauthorised access.

Example: a strong password + MFA (good security) can still sit beside a public phone number, birthday and live location (weak privacy). Strong security does not equal strong privacy.

Digital footprint

Active: what you deliberately share (posts, comments, profile fields). Passive: data generated or collected through online activity, depending on the service. This tool looks only at self-reported practices such as reviewing old posts and unused accounts.

How oversharing raises social-engineering risk

Public details such as employer, college, travel plans, family references and interests can give a scammer believable context. Reducing unnecessary public information, verifying unexpected requests through a second channel, and never sharing verification codes all make such scams harder.

Why location matters

Real-time location and routines show where you are and where you are not. Posting after you have left a place exposes less than broadcasting while you are there.
